## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads ensure that projects meet security, privacy, and regulatory requirements while balancing delivery speed and operational risk.

### Responsibilities
- Review features and architectures for security and compliance risks
- Define security checkpoints, review gates, and remediation plans
- Partner with engineering, PMs, and stakeholders to ensure controls are in place
- Coordinate with legal, privacy, or compliance stakeholders as needed
- Monitor vulnerabilities, risk exposure, and remediation status

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Protect customer trust and organizational standards
- Integrate secure practices without slowing delivery unnecessarily

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and risk assessments
- Dependency tracking with PMs and engineering leads
- Policy and compliance updates to stakeholders

### How this role interacts with others
- Works with Developers on secure implementation and validation
- Partners with Product Managers on risk trade-offs and release readiness
- Coordinates with Project Managers on escalation and mitigation tracking
- Supports the PM and Sponsor on compliance-sensitive decisions

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads define quality expectations, test strategy, and validation plans so that delivered work meets agreed acceptance criteria and user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and acceptance validation approach
- Coordinate quality planning across sprint and release activities
- Review defects, test coverage, and release readiness signals
- Partner with Developers and Product Managers on testability and quality gates
- Ensure critical workflows are validated before release

### Goals
- Deliver high-confidence releases
- Reduce defects and costly rework
- Align quality standards with user and business expectations

### Typical Communication
- Sprint quality reviews and defect triage
- Acceptance criteria validation with Product Managers
- Release readiness discussions with PMs and stakeholders

### How this role interacts with others
- Works closely with Developers on test design and defect resolution
- Aligns with Product Managers on customer-facing acceptance criteria
- Supports Project Managers with milestone and release quality checkpoints
- Escalates product or quality risks to PMs and leadership when needed

## Release Manager / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the movement of work from validated implementation to deployment, helping teams reduce operational disruption and improve rollout confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Manage release plans, sequencing, and deployment windows
- Coordinate pre-release checks, rollback planning, and communication
- Partner with engineering and QA to validate readiness
- Monitor post-deployment risks and support issue triage
- Communicate release status and known issues to stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational disruption
- Improve rollout predictability and stakeholder confidence
- Support smooth transitions from development to production

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Stakeholder updates before and after launches
- Incident and rollback coordination when needed

### How this role interacts with others
- Coordinates with Developers and QA on deployment readiness
- Aligns with Project Managers on timelines and communication plans
- Works with Product Managers on launch messaging and stakeholder expectations
- Escalates operational issues to sponsors or leadership during critical releases

## Customer Success / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Support Liaisons ensure that customer feedback, support realities, and adoption concerns shape planning and delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer pain points, feature requests, and support trends
- Represent user support impact in backlog prioritization and planning
- Partner with Product and Project teams on readiness and rollout communication
- Help identify training, documentation, or adoption issues
- Track post-release customer impact and feedback loops

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure delivery reflects real user and support needs
- Reduce avoidable friction after release

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback sessions and support summaries
- Stakeholder readiness discussions
- Release notes and adoption follow-up

### How this role interacts with others
- Feeds Product Managers with customer and support insights
- Collaborates with Project Managers on communication and launch readiness
- Supports Developers by clarifying real-world usage scenarios and edge cases
- Helps PMs and leadership assess feature value and adoption impact

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads define how success is measured, how outcomes are evaluated, and how evidence informs future iterations.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Establish measurement plans for feature adoption, performance, and business impact
- Partner with Product and PM teams on decision-making based on evidence
- Validate instrumentation, data quality, and analysis assumptions
- Help teams evaluate whether outcomes met expected goals

### Goals
- Make outcomes measurable and transparent
- Support data-informed prioritization and iteration
- Improve the quality of decisions after launch

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and dashboard reviews
- Weekly or milestone-based performance updates
- Decision support for roadmap and retrospective discussions

### How this role interacts with others
- Works with Product Managers to define success metrics and outcomes
- Supports Project Managers with status reporting and milestone evidence
- Helps Developers ensure instrumentation and observability are built in
- Collaborates with stakeholders to communicate impact and business value

## Architecture / Platform Lead

### Role Summary
Architecture / Platform Leads define technical direction, integration constraints, and platform standards so projects can be delivered with clarity and scalability.

### Responsibilities
- Define system architecture and technical guardrails
- Identify dependencies, integration points, and cross-team constraints
- Review design trade-offs for scalability, maintainability, and reliability
- Guide platform decisions and technical standards
- Support risk mitigation for architecture-driven issues

### Goals
- Align technical decisions with product and business goals
- Reduce avoidable complexity and integration risk
- Improve long-term maintainability and operational resilience

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Platform dependency tracking and technical risk reviews
- Cross-team coordination on integration work

### How this role interacts with others
- Advises Developers on design quality and technical constraints
- Supports Product Managers and PMs with sequencing and dependency planning
- Coordinates with Security and QA on architecture-related risk and validation
- Helps leadership assess technical feasibility and long-term platform impact
